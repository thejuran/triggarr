<objective>
Close the live-verified Sonarr v5 attribution gap found in Plan 81-07: v5's grab history carries `episodeId`/`seriesId` but no nested `episode`, so no v5 grab is ever credited. Add an episode-ID fallback with series verification, cached per tracking check and bounded in call volume, and replace the schema-derived v5 fixture with a sanitized real v5 capture. Missing or conflicting identity still gets no credit.

Purpose: Owner decision (81-LIVE-MATRIX.md "Owner review (v5)", 2026-10-19), quoted: "When history lacks season information, resolve its episodeId through Sonarr's episode endpoint and verify the series matches. Cache that lookup within each tracking check. If identity still cannot be established, skip credit rather than guess. Replace the schema-derived fixture with a sanitized real v5 response." This plan is the code half; Plan 81-11 repeats the failed live rows on the fixed build.
Output: live-captured sanitized v5 fixtures, honest relabelling of synthesized fixtures, `SonarrClient.get_episode`, the tracking fallback, and real-client-path regression tests. TDD: failing-test commit first.
