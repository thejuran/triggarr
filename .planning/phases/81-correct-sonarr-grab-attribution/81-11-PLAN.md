<objective>
Implement the owner's directed fix for the live v5 gap (81-LIVE-MATRIX.md "Owner review (v5)"): "When history lacks season information, resolve its episodeId through Sonarr's episode endpoint and verify the series matches. Cache that lookup within each tracking check. If identity still cannot be established, skip credit rather than guess." TDD: failing-test commit first.

Purpose: on Sonarr v5 5.0.0.2701 no grab is ever credited because history omits the nested episode. Correct v5 attribution is an acceptance requirement (SONARRV5-02).
Design choice (Claude's discretion, plan check 3): resolve from one series episode list per group per check, with per-id lookups only as a fallback. Both are Sonarr's episode endpoint; the list form keeps request volume at one call per series however large the pack, so no budget can starve a large pack.
Output: `SonarrClient.get_series_episodes` and `get_episode`, bounded per-check resolution in Sonarr tracking, and real-client-path regressions against the 81-10 captures.
</objective>
